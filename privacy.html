Privacy

Last updated 13 October 2026

This website is a single information page for Hybrid Race Coaching. It is designed to collect as little as possible.

What this site collects

Nothing, directly. There are no forms, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The site sets no cookies and loads no resources from third parties: fonts are served from this site, not from Google or anyone else.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub may record the IP address of visitors in its server logs for security purposes. We do not have access to any personal information from those logs. GitHub's handling of that data is described in the GitHub privacy statement.

If you email us

If you write to hybridracecoaching@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you send. We use it only to reply to you, we do not add you to any list, and we do not share it. Email is handled by Google (Gmail) under its own terms.

Links to other sites

The site links to our Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and X accounts. Those platforms have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave this site.

Questions

Hybrid Race Coaching is operated in Ontario, Canada, and this page is written with Canada's PIPEDA in mind. For any question about your personal information, email hybridracecoaching@gmail.com.
