Accessibility

Hybrid Race Coaching wants everyone to be able to use this site. It is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

What that means here

Text is real text, not images, so it can be resized and read by screen readers. The logo carries a text description. Colours meet contrast requirements. Every link can be reached and operated with a keyboard, and the keyboard focus is visible. Motion is reduced for people who have asked their device for that. The page works at any screen size and at 400% zoom.

Tell us if something is in the way

If any part of this site is hard to use, email hybridracecoaching@gmail.com and say what happened and what device or software you were using. We will fix what we can and reply within ten business days.

This statement was prepared on 13 October 2026 and will be updated as the site changes.
